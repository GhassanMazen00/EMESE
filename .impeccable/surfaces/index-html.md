## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a garment factory's tech pack for the customer's own order: every product shown as a spec sheet with its print spot measured and labelled. It refuses the category default of a lifestyle hero with a slider of stock photos and same-size product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white sheet (#fbfbfa) and pure white plates on a faint 8 mm grid, ink #141414, hairline rules at 1px, square corners (2px max). Callouts are thin leader lines ending in a dot, with a measurement in IBM Plex Mono ("تطريز · 9 cm"). CMY only as three small registration marks and as the active technique tick. Product photos sit in framed plates with corner crop marks. Buttons are flat ink rectangles.

STORY: A buyer sees their kind of order (uniforms, kitchen, office gifts, events, safety, brand trims), sees exactly how and where the logo goes, believes EMESE does all techniques in-house, then tries a logo in the studio or sends a quote on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "إنت تتخيّل، وإحنا ننفّذ." on the start side at about 4.5rem with a short lead, the quote button and the WhatsApp number. On the end side a large spec plate: one product photo with two callouts drawn in, cycling every 5s through five products; a strip of five thumbnails under it. The techniques strip sits at the fold.

FORM: tech pack, position 3 on the ordered list; seed:b446b1ece2bf0e19

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
