# Product photos to make with ChatGPT

**21 photos are needed** for the site (19 products, plus 5 optional extras for the hat collection).
They are used on the homepage photo grid, the product cards, the social posts, the WhatsApp catalog and
the link preview.

## How to make them

1. Make every product **blank: no logo, no text, no brand**. I put the EMESE logo on afterwards, with the
   real technique for each product (stitched thread for embroidery, ink in the folds for print, burned or
   cut in for engraving). Logos made by AI come out with broken letters.
2. Paste the **style line** at the end of every prompt, so all the photos match as one set.
3. Ask for **square** images (1:1), the largest size it offers.
4. If ChatGPT adds a logo, text or a person anyway, say: *"Same image, but completely blank: no logo, no
   text, no label."*
5. Save each photo with the **file name** in the table (e.g. `polo.png`) and upload them here, all at once
   or a few at a time.

### Style line (add to every prompt)

```
Professional studio product photograph, photorealistic. The whole product is visible, centred, filling
about 70% of the frame. Plain seamless warm light-grey background, soft diffused light from the upper
left, a soft natural shadow under the product. Realistic material texture, sharp focus, no logo, no text,
no brand marks, no people, no other objects. Square image, 1:1.
```

## Good to know

- **Colours:** these match the current site (navy, white, black and natural). If you want other colours
  for some products, change the colour in the prompt; keep a few light and a few dark.
- **Angles matter:** the logo needs a clear, flat-ish area facing the camera (the "Logo goes" column).
  If a photo hides that area (a crumpled shirt, the mug's handle in front), make it again.
- **The studio is separate:** these photos are for the homepage, cards, posts and catalog. The studio
  keeps its 3D clothes and its current previews.
