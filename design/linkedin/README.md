# EMESE LinkedIn company page: everything to fill in

Create it at **linkedin.com/company/setup/new** (you need a personal LinkedIn account, signed in).
Choose **Company**, then fill the form with the fields below. Items marked **YOU** are facts only you know.

```
EMESE makes custom supplies for companies in Egypt: printed and embroidered uniforms, caps of every kind, notebooks, engraved pens, flags, cards, mugs and corporate gifts, all carrying your logo.
```
